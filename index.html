Fintech Executive & Founder

Two Decades Building Financial Products Across Three Regions

Mikaal Abdulla has worked across nearly every layer of the fintech stack: technology consulting at Deloitte, a decade of regional expansion at E*TRADE, and company building as the founder of 8 Securities. Few fintech professionals carry experience in all three, and that range shapes how he approaches product and market strategy.

Under his leadership, 8 Securities launched as Asia's first mobile-only brokerage and went on to introduce the region's first robo-advisor, social trading platform, and subscription pricing model. The story of building Asia's first mobile-only brokerage is a study in market timing and product conviction.

Abdulla has lived and worked in Silicon Valley, New York, London, Dubai, Mumbai, Tokyo, Singapore, Bangkok, and Hong Kong. That geographic breadth runs through every phase of Mikaal Abdulla's fintech career, from E*TRADE's 14-market expansion to the products he launched in Asia.
